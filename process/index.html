A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked
Questions

Every requirement is different.
Let’s start with the essentials.

What products can I source through Arpit Impex?+

You can enquire about locks, door handles, hinges, door accessories and architectural hardware. Product availability is confirmed for each enquiry.

Can I enquire about a product not listed on the website?+

Yes. Share a description or reference with us so we can review possible sourcing options. The catalogue is a starting point, not a confirmed availability list.

What information should I share to receive a quotation?+

Please share your product requirements, specifications, estimated quantity and destination country. Reference details and packaging requirements are helpful too.

Can you discuss bulk and wholesale requirements?+

Yes. We can discuss bulk and wholesale enquiries. Minimum order quantities and pricing depend on the product and supplier.

Can you help coordinate international shipping and export documentation?+

We can discuss export documentation and shipping coordination as part of your enquiry. Arrangements and responsibilities depend on the agreed order and trade terms.

Do you accept custom product specifications or packaging requests?+

Share your requirements for review. Customisation, materials, finishes and packaging depend on supplier capabilities and order quantities.
